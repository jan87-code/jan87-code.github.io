Privacy Policy

This application is used exclusively for personal Google Drive synchronization through the open-source software rclone.

Access to Google Drive is authorized by the account owner using Google OAuth.

Files are synchronized between the owner's personal computers and their Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the rclone configuration.

This application does not operate an independent server and does not collect user data for advertising, analytics, or resale.

This application is not available to third-party users.
